# Handling research evidence

Keep original reports, their digests, source identifiers and review history in a project-controlled private workspace. Record what the author reported and what a separate reviewer reproduced. Preserve unsuccessful attempts and limitations.

When documenting a pattern publicly, author a new fictional fixture with its own identifier and provenance. Inspect its values, references and paths before setting its publication-review flag. Keep the original record intact. A local kit export does not upload material to ChatGPT.
